How to play

It's classic Pong against a clever AI, with deuce rules borrowed from tennis.

Controls

Move paddleMouse or trackpad over the court; drag on touch screens
ServeClick the court or Space
Pause / resumeSpace or the Pause button
PowerupsW Speed · D Size, or click them
Restart / QuitButtons beside the court

Tennis-style deuce

First to 7 points wins a game. At 6–6 it's deuce: win a point to take the Advantage, then win the next one to take the game. Lose the point on advantage and it's back to deuce, as many times as it takes. The scoreboard shows how many game or match points you hold.

Match formats

One-shotA single game
Best of 3First to 2 games
Best of 5First to 3 games
Best of 7First to 4 games

Powerups

You get two per game and can use one per point, while the ball is in play. Effects last until the point ends.

  • W Speed: your paddle reacts 4× faster and the AI slows to 60%.
  • D Size: your paddle grows to 160%.

Powerups are disabled in Extreme.

Difficulty

EasyMostly chases the ball, gentle angles, tires quickly.
MediumReads part of the ball's path and goes for moderate angles.
HardHighly predictive, hunts corners and sets up awkward returns.
ExtremeFastest ball and a relentless AI. No powerups.

At every level the AI tires as a rally gets longer, so keep the pressure on.

Trajectory visualizer

Toggle the dashed guide to see where the ball will reach the AI side, including wall bounces. It uses the same prediction the AI does, which makes it great for learning angles.

Leaderboard

Every finished match is saved to the leaderboard.

Themes

Choose Neon, Retro, Synthwave or Arctic before a match, from the menu or the header. The theme locks and the pickers hide once the match starts; they come back when it ends.

Hitting angles

Where the ball meets your paddle sets its return angle: hit near the edges for steep shots. The ball also speeds up the longer a game goes on.
